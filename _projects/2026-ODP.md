<p>
  This project page the key deliverables from my Intro To Mechanical Design ODP (Open Design Project). Spotted lanternflies have posed a significant challenge over the last decade or so since their arrival as an invasive species in the US. A major target of the lanternflies has been grape farms. For our class project, my group and I were tasked with creating a device to combat the spotted lanternflies on grape farms.
